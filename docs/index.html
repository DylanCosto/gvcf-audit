Enough evidence to tell?

A gVCF can report a confident reference genotype, a variant genotype, or insufficient evidence. gvcf-audit checks the recorded evidence against your quality requirements and shows which positions remain unresolved.

These are actual tool outputs generated from the small fictional dataset in the repository. They do not describe a real person's genome.

1. Explore an audit

The supplied gene targets cover 750 unique bases. Of those, 450 (60%) meet the requirements. The other 300 have low depth, missing genotypes, missing records or ambiguous reference bases.

Open the report to see the reasons, search the two example genes and inspect the recorded genotype fields.

2. Compare stricter requirements

The second audit uses the same input and targets, but raises minimum genotype quality from 20 to 40. 200 bases lose callability; 250 remain callable. The underlying data has not changed.

The comparison shows exactly which positions and gene/exon summaries changed.

View the stricter audit

Callable does not mean proven correct. This checks evidence reported in the gVCF. It cannot reconstruct missed variants or establish biological accuracy. The reference FASTA checks reference agreement, not which variants should exist in the sample.
